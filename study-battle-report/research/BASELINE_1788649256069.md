## Data still required

1. Three independent sessions in each of eight controlled cells: actual
   displayed incoming reduction around 0–10%, 40–55%, 70–80%, and at least 90%,
   each both without and with resistance (24 qualifying reports).
2. Equal-total component tests: one large reduction versus two smaller
   reductions, plus reversed application order; three sessions per setup.
3. For every report: game build/season, hero and tactic levels, equipment and
   strategy, formation/row, pre-battle attributes, full loadouts, and supply.
4. Prefer non-mirror lineups, non-critical first-round hits, stable attacker and
   target, and non-lethal observations.

Until those gaps are filled, the exact base-damage function, modifier channels,
resistance composition, and near-cap behaviour remain unidentifiable.
